# Bōk

A native iOS client for [Audiobookshelf](https://audiobookshelf.org), 
a free self-hosted audiobook server.

## Requirements

- A running Audiobookshelf server (v2.x or later)
- iOS 17 or later

## Support

Open an [issue](https://github.com/jjcxdev/bok/issues) and describe 
what you're seeing. Include your iOS version and Audiobookshelf server 
version if possible.

## Privacy Policy

Bōk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any 
third-party server. All data — your server address, credentials, and 
listening history — is stored locally on your device or on your own 
Audiobookshelf server, which you control entirely. Bōk contains no 
analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**  
No account with any third party is needed. You connect directly to your 
own Audiobookshelf server using your server's credentials.

**Can I use it without Tailscale?**  
Yes. Any publicly accessible Audiobookshelf server works over HTTP or HTTPS.

**Where is my data stored?**  
On your device and your server. Nowhere else.
